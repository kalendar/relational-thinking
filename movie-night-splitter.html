Movie Night Splitter

The Student Activities Board runs free movie nights all over campus and tracks every ticket claimed in one spreadsheet. It works fine — until a theater's building gets typed two different ways on two different rows. Your job: split this one table into smaller tables so every fact is stored in exactly one place, then show how the new tables connect. This one's the trickiest yet — it takes four tables, and one of the dependencies is hiding inside another one.

How to play: Drag a column name (or click it, then click a table) into a table below. Click ★ on a column to make it that table's key — the column that never repeats. Once two tables have keys, use Link to another table to connect them. No ER diagram needed — we're doing that next week.
