In this folder we're writing a textbook for an Introduction to Databases class for undergraduate business students at Marshall University.

The textbook takes a concepts-first approach, helping students build a solid conceptual base before moving on to technical topics like writing SQL queries.

The textbook should be written at a 10th grade level. 

Examples should be drawn from topics of interest to undergraduates between 18-25 years old, including music, concerts, sports, movies and TV, Netflix and Spotify, memes, college life, etc.
